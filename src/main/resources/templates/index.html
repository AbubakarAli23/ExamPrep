How it works

Four steps from a handout to a revision routine.

ExamPrep keeps the workflow deliberately small. Your document is the source; everything else is built from it.

01

Upload

Choose a supported academic document. The file is validated and stored privately, outside any public URL.

02

Extract

Text is extracted with page and slide references where they genuinely exist. Scanned PDFs are reported clearly instead of guessed.

03

Generate

Gemini analyses the extracted material in bounded sections and assembles a complete study package.

04

Study

Revise, flip flashcards, answer practice questions, sit a timed mock exam and ask the tutor about the same material.

Study tools

A complete package, not a single summary.

Each study package is organised the way revision actually happens: understand, recall, practise, then test under time pressure.

  • Revision notes

    Organised, readable notes that preserve the technical meaning of the original material.

  • Definitions and formulas

    A glossary of terms and a reference section for formulas that actually appear in your document.

  • Flashcards

    Front-and-back cards for active recall, with next, previous and reveal controls.

  • Practice questions

    Multiple choice, true/false and short answer questions with suggested answers and explanations.

  • Timed mock examination

    A countdown, automatic scoring and a full review of what went wrong. Answers stay on the server until you submit.

  • Tutor with sources

    Ask about a concept and see which pages or slides the answer came from. When the material is silent, it says so.

Privacy and responsibility

Your documents stay private to your account.

Uploaded files are stored outside the public web root, behind per-student ownership checks. You can delete a document and its study packages at any time from your workspace.

To generate study material, the extracted text is sent to Google's Gemini API. ExamPrep never exposes your API key or your documents to the browser, and it does not log document contents.

Built for honest study

Generated content is educational assistance. ExamPrep labels what came from your document, discards citations that do not match a real page or slide, and refuses to invent formulas. Check anything important against your original notes.
